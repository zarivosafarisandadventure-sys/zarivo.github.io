Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from our travelers.

What is the best time to visit Kenya?+
Kenya can be visited throughout the year. The dry seasons are popular for wildlife viewing, while the Great Wildebeest Migration is generally associated with the middle and later part of the year.
How many days should I spend on safari?+
The ideal safari length depends on your destinations and interests. Short trips can focus on one destination, while longer trips allow you to combine several parks and experiences.
Can you customize a safari?+
Yes. We can help create customized safari itineraries based on your destinations, travel dates, activities, group size and budget.
Do you organize airport transfers?+
Yes. Airport transfers can be arranged as part of your travel package.
How do I book a safari?+
You can contact us through WhatsApp, phone or email. You can also use the booking form on this website.
